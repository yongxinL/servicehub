# Product

## Purpose
Records product intent: the problem, users, goals, non-goals, product-level requirements, constraints, and success measures.

## Authority
`vision.md` and `requirements.md`, once created and approved, are authoritative for product intent. They are not authoritative for feature behaviour (native specifications) or technical design (plans and ADRs). Owning chapter: central manual 08.

## Artifact triggers
Create both at adoption (new projects) or when product intent is first formalized (existing projects). Update on material product change, with owner approval.

## Naming and identifiers
Fixed filenames `vision.md` and `requirements.md`. Product requirements may use local `PR-NNN` labels for reference; they are not governed identifiers.

## Status expectations
Draft, In Review, Approved, Superseded.
